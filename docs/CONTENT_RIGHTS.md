# Content and rights policy

## What is bundled

All 12 track records, artist names, album names, release dates and sonic descriptions in `DEMO_SONGS` are explicitly fictional examples. Similarity to existing names is coincidental and does not establish a credit, affiliation, endorsement or release fact. There is no real A.R. Rahman entry, no scraped catalogue and no commercial lyric excerpt. Cover artwork is original CSS geometry. Icons come from Lucide (ISC).

The four-line lyric exercise, English text, Tamil/Hindi/Telugu versions and their Latin renderings were created specifically as project demonstration writing. The exercise is deliberately reused for multiple fictional tracks; it is not twelve claimed real songs. Timestamps are artificial practice timing, not alignment to recordings. The displayed years are synthetic catalogue values. Translations and transliterations should receive a native-editor quality pass before a real release.

## Real catalogue publication gate

1. Identify the composition, recording, performers and lyricist using stable IDs; document authoritative source URLs and retrieval dates. Do not infer a singer/composer credit from the track title.
2. Obtain the relevant text-display licence. A music streaming link, public metadata record, attribution, paid audio subscription or internet availability is **not** permission to reproduce lyrics.
3. Verify separate rights to translate, transliterate, time/synchronise text, display artwork, and play any audio. Do not assume translated or romanised text escapes copyright.
4. Record rights holder, signed evidence/reference, approved territories, dates/expiry, commercial use, attribution and limitations. Keep contracts private on the server; public exports should contain reference IDs rather than confidential agreements.
5. Have a human reviewer verify each lyric variant, language tag and credit. Store revisions, reviewer and timestamps. Mark unknown fields as unknown; disable filters without evidence.
6. Build an operator-owned contact/takedown route and a logged review process before public real-content publication. No fake email address is included here.
7. Rights status `licensed` in an imported file is an assertion from its uploader, **not verification**. This app never publishes the import to a shared backend.

## User files

Users may practice with their own local audio and LRC if they have permission. This does not grant public distribution, derivative-work or synchronisation rights. Files remain in the reader’s memory/object URLs, are not uploaded, and are discarded when the reader closes. Saved favourites/collections do not save media.
